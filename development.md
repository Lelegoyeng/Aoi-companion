## 📝 Step-by-Step Development Guide

### Step 1: Prepare the 3D Anime Character
1. Create or download your 3D anime character model (e.g., using **VRoid Studio**, **Mixamo**, or **Sketchfab**).
2. Export the character model in `.glb` or `.gltf` format with animations (*Idle*, *Talking*, *Waving*).
3. Place your model file inside the `public/models/` directory:
   ```text
   public/
   └── models/
       └── character.glb
